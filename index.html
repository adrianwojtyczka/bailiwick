Bailiwick

A settlement-building strategy game, in the spirit of The Settlers II

✦

You do not command an army — you command an economy. Claim territory, lay the roads that carry every plank and loaf, and watch a handful of huts grow into a province that feeds itself.

What you will be doing

  • Roads first

    Nothing moves without a road. Every path you draw becomes a supply line, and every junction a place where goods pile up or run dry.

  • Production chains

    Bread needs flour, flour needs grain, grain needs a farm with room to grow. Break one link and the whole province notices.

  • Borders & banners

    Territory is won by building, not by marching. Raise a watchtower and your border creeps outward over the land it can see.

  • Land that matters

    Forest, water, meadow and mountain each decide what can stand on them. The map is not scenery — it is the puzzle.
